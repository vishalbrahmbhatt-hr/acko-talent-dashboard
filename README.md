# Talent Index Framework — Interactive Prototype

A web-based prototype that visualises the **Talent Index Framework (TIF)** — a four-pillar model for identifying, differentiating, and ring-fencing critical talent in modern, journey-led organisations.

Built as a working prototype to translate framework thinking into a tool a CHRO can actually look at, not just a deck.

## What the framework does

TIF moves talent decisions away from a single performance rating toward a composite view across four dimensions:

- **Performance** — current delivery against role
- **Potential** — capacity to take on a step-change in scope
- **Trajectory** — direction and velocity of growth over the last 12–24 months
- **Fungibility** — ability to operate across functional boundaries, especially in POD or cross-functional structures

The four pillars are designed to surface the small percentage of talent that drives a disproportionate share of business outcomes — and to make merit allocation decisions easier to defend.

## What the dashboard does

- Visualises talent distribution across the four pillars
- Highlights the mission-critical cohort (top decile)
- Shows merit budget allocation logic — value-based distribution vs uniform spread
- Allows filtering by function, level, and tenure band

All data in this prototype is **synthetic and illustrative** — no employee data, no real org information.

## Stack

- HTML, CSS, JavaScript (single-file prototype)
- Designed for rapid iteration and stakeholder demos
